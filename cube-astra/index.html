How to play

Follow the arrowhead

Tap an arrow to send it toward the edge of its face. If it hits another arrow, both turn red and yours slides back. Clear every arrow to finish.

Look around the cube

Drag to spin. Pinch to zoom and slide two fingers to pan. Straighten faces the nearest side toward you. The arrow buttons turn one face at a time.

Take as many tries as you like

Hint turns to a free arrow. Undo brings your last arrow back. Restart plays the same cube again.
